Go

Surround stones to capture them and claim territory. You play black and move first.

How to play

Click an intersection to place a black stone. A stone or connected group with no empty neighbouring points is captured. You can’t make a move that leaves your own group without liberties, unless it captures, and you can’t repeat an earlier board position. When you have nothing useful left to play, press Pass. The game ends when both sides pass. Each side scores its stones plus the empty areas it alone surrounds, and white gets 6.5 points of komi to make up for moving second.
